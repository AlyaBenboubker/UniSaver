# Roadmap

## Lot 1 - Documents

FileImporter, local storage, security-scoped bookmarks, PDFKit, integrated PDF viewer, reading progress, and PDF plus notes side by side.

## Lot 2 - Notes

Rich editor, autosave, links to course/chapter/session, PencilKit, and annotations.

## Lot 3 - Planner

Tasks, assignments, exams, calendar, notifications, and revision plans.

## Lot 4 - Study Studio

Flashcards, QCM, quiz, bulk import, spaced repetition, and error tracking.

## Lot 5 - Conversational AI

ChatGPT-style conversation, bulk flashcard and QCM creation, source selection, structured generation, preview, bulk editing, validation before saving, PDF citations, and quizzes generated from errors.

## Lot 6 - Recordings

AVFoundation recording, audio import, transcription, chaptering, summaries, and revision generation.

## Lot 7 - Sync

CloudKit or remote backend, offline-first behavior, conflict resolution, and Mac/iPad synchronization.
