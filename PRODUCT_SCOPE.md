# Product Scope

## Lot 0

Lot 0 creates the native foundation and first usable academic workflow:

- Today dashboard.
- Classes workspace.
- Course creation and editing.
- Course detail workspace.
- Chapter creation, editing, reordering, archiving, and deletion confirmation.
- Session creation, editing, chapter selection, chronological sorting, progress status, and deletion confirmation.
- SwiftData persistence.
- Preview/demo data isolated from real storage.

## Explicitly Out Of Scope

- PDF import and reading.
- Advanced notes.
- Audio recording and transcription.
- AI chat or generation.
- Cloud sync.
- Notifications.
- Planner tasks, assignments, and exams beyond placeholders.
- Flashcards, QCM, quiz, and spaced repetition.

## Design Direction

The current direction is Digital Study Desk: calm, dense, warm, native, premium, and useful. It avoids marketing landing pages, mascots, floating widgets, and decorative inactive buttons.
